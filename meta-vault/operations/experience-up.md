# Experience up

**When:** «опыт наверх», the monthly review of agents at Maksi, before a new version.

One page for the original — `meta-vault/experience/<YYYY-MM>.md`, born with the first one:

1. **What worked** — operations used, how often (from `log.md` and the lots' folders).
2. **What broke** — errors of scripts, platforms that changed, places where the agent stumbled.
3. **Asked for, not built** — her words verbatim with dates (also in [[../structure|structure]]
   §Asked for, not built).
4. **Lessons for the template** — a line each.

**No client data**: no lots' contents, no firms' figures, no BINs, no names of people. Only how the
agent behaved. The page travels up with the repository; Скандар lifts it into the original.
